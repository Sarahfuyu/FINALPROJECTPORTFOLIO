DELIGHTFUL TREATS


WELCOME

Hello! my name is Sarah Campos, and I am the owner of my home bakery "Delightful Treats".

WHAT WE SELL

We have your typical baked goods, muffins, cakes, brownies, cookies, and much more, though our main speciality is our jumbo muffins.

WHAT MAKES US SPECIAL

Everything we bake is allergy free. We have gluten free, dairy free, vegan, no GMO, no soy, no seed oils and much more! My goal for my bakery is for it to be the first ever allergy free bakery in the Bronx.

WHY I STARTED

I've always had a sweet tooth, until the day I got diagnosed with PCOS. I couldn't eat certain foods anymore, sweets being the main one, so I made it my goal to make sweets that not only I can enjoy, but for women in similar situations to enjoy too.
